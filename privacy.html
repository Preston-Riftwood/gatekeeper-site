Gatekeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Who Gatekeeper is for

Gatekeeper is a private parental control system used by one household on its own computers and phones. There are no accounts for anyone else, and it is not offered to the public.

What Google data is accessed

  • The Gatekeeper app accesses only the files it creates in the owner's Google Drive (the Google Drive "drive.file" permission). It cannot see any other files.
  • The developer's build tool (rclone) accesses the owner's Google Drive to sync one project folder of documents and reports.

How the data is used

Only to sync settings, activity logs, and project files between the family's own devices. It is used for nothing else.

Storage and protection

Gatekeeper's sync files are encrypted on the family's own devices before they are uploaded, so Google stores only encrypted data. Project documents synced by the build tool are ordinary files in the owner's own Drive.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. There is no advertising and no analytics. No third party is involved other than Google as the storage provider.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gatekeeper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

The owner can revoke Gatekeeper's access at any time from the third-party connections page of their Google Account, and can delete the Gatekeeper folder from their Drive.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
