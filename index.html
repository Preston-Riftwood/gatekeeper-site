Gatekeeper

Gatekeeper is a private, non-commercial parental control system built by one family for its own home computers. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does with Google

Gatekeeper keeps its own settings and activity files in the owner's Google Drive, so the family's phone and home computers stay in sync. The developer's build tooling also syncs one project folder in the same Drive.

Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising. The details are in the privacy policy.
